What this site is

Fellowship Time Off lets fellows of the USC Transplant Surgery Fellowship request and manage time off on the fellowship's shared Google Calendar. It is run by the fellowship for its own members.

What we get when you sign in with Google

  • Your email address and name, from Google sign-in. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • We use your email only to check that you are on the fellowship's list (and whether you are an administrator). We don't store it, sell it or share it.
  • Your sign-in is kept in your browser for the current tab only, and expires within an hour. "Sign out" removes it.

What is stored

  • Time off you request (for example "Name - Weekend") and the matching cross-cover, as events on the fellowship's Google Calendar, visible to the fellowship.
  • This site has no database, no analytics and no advertising, and sets no tracking cookies.

What this site is

The USC Transplant Surgery Fellowship runs three sites for its own members: time off (requesting and managing time off on the fellowship's shared Google Calendar), the call schedule, and the portal (the member list). This page covers all three.

What we get when you sign in

  • With Google: your email address and name. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • With an emailed code: your email address. The code is only used to check the address is yours and is forgotten within minutes.
  • We use your email only to check that you are on the fellowship's member list and what you may do on each site. We don't sell it or share it.
  • Your sign-in is kept in your browser (by the portal, so one sign-in works on all three sites). It expires after an hour (Google) or a week (emailed code). "Sign out" on any of the sites removes it.

What is stored

  • Time off you request (for example "Name - Weekend") and the matching cross-cover, and living donor cases added on the call schedule, as events on the fellowship's Google Calendars, visible to the fellowship.
  • The member list, kept by the fellowship's administrators: each member's name, email addresses, phone numbers, graduating year or job title, and roles on the sites. Members can see it in the portal.
  • If you sign up, the details you send (name, email, phone, graduating year or job title, and any note) are kept until an administrator approves or declines them.
  • Which automatic emails you get, and at which of your addresses (you choose in the portal), and when you first signed in.
  • A record of activity on the sites: when you sign in with an emailed code and sign out, which sites you open, and the changes you make (or try to make). Only the most recent activity is kept (usually about two months), and only the fellowship's super administrators can see it. Looking around the sites isn't recorded.
  • Fellows' email addresses are added to the sharing settings of the fellowship's Google Calendars, so they can see them.
  • The sites have no analytics and no advertising, and set no tracking cookies.

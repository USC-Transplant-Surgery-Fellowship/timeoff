What this site is

The USC Transplant Surgery Fellowship runs three sites for its own members: time off (requesting and managing time off on the fellowship's shared Google Calendar), the call schedule, and the portal (the member list). This page covers all three.

What we get when you sign in with Google

  • Your email address and name, from Google sign-in. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • We use your email only to check that you are on the fellowship's member list and what you may do on each site. We don't sell it or share it.
  • Your sign-in is kept in your browser (by the portal, so one sign-in works on all three sites) and expires within an hour. "Sign out" on any of the sites removes it.

What is stored

  • Time off you request (for example "Name - Weekend") and the matching cross-cover, and living donor cases added on the call schedule, as events on the fellowship's Google Calendars, visible to the fellowship.
  • The member list, kept by the fellowship's administrators: each member's name, email addresses, phone numbers, graduating year or job title, and roles on the sites. Members can see it in the portal.
  • The sites have no analytics and no advertising, and set no tracking cookies.
